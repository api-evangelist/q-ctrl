# Q-CTRL

> Q-CTRL builds infrastructure software to power the quantum future — AI-driven quantum control, error suppression, and performance-management tools spanning quantum computing and quantum sensing. Products are consumed through official Python clients that call the Q-CTRL cloud (a hosted GraphQL API); there is no public REST/OpenAPI surface.

## Products
- [Boulder Opal](https://docs.q-ctrl.com/boulder-opal/get-started): Python toolkit to automate and improve the performance of quantum computing and sensing hardware.
- [Fire Opal](https://docs.q-ctrl.com/fire-opal/get-started): Error-suppression software that improves quantum-algorithm results.
- [Black Opal](https://q-ctrl.com/black-opal): Interactive quantum-education platform.
- [Ironstone Opal](https://q-ctrl.com/ironstone-opal): GPS-free quantum navigation system.
